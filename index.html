About the Club

A friendly club with a big heart

Kippax Amateur Swimming Club (ASC) is a not-for-profit swimming club, established in 1975, with around 100 members. We are a small, friendly, competitive club and we are extremely proud of all our many achievements.

The club is run entirely by volunteers, with members from a wide area including Kippax, Garforth, Sherburn in Elmet, Woodlesford, Methley, Pontefract and beyond.

We have swimmers of all levels — from beginners to those competing at county and regional level — ranging in age from 4 to 17. We train weekdays at Kippax and Rothwell Leisure Centres.

  • 🏊 Learn-to-swim through to competitive squads
  • 🏅 County & regional level competition
  • 🤝 Welcoming, family-friendly community
  • 💙 Not-for-profit & volunteer led
